Rane SL 4 CoreAudio Driver

Version @VERSION@ • Native for Apple Silicon (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4) & Intel

Original idea and project by: pepelee (PPLEE)
Instagram: @pepeleedj — instagram.com/pepeleedj/

This installer sets up the low-latency driver for the Rane SL 4 audio interface on your Mac:

  • 10 inputs and 10 outputs (Decks 1-4 and Aux) at 24-bit / 48 or 96 kHz, with a low-latency audio engine. The sample rate is chosen on the SL 4 itself, with its 48/96 kHz control, and the driver follows it. The channel order (1-2 Deck 1 … 7-8 Deck 4, 9-10 Aux) has not yet been verified on an SL 4.
  • Automatic USB / Thru switching of decks 1-4 when you open and close your audio software (Ableton Live, Traktor, Mixxx, Serato, Logic Pro, etc.).
  • SL 4 Control Panel app (in Applications) and the sl4panel utility: Thru on each deck, output volume and levels, and the box's sample rate and USB port. (The SL 4 does not report its Phono/Line switches, so they are not shown.)
  • Serato DJ Pro 2.5.5: includes SL4Api.framework (DVS modes ABS / REL / INT and THRU).
  • Works straight away: no restart needed, and no need to turn off System Integrity Protection (SIP).
